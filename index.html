About

I am a full-time Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Dhaka, and an M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). I also work as a remote research intern with Dr. Tarikul Islam Papon at UMass Boston.

My research is on data systems. Right now I work on LSM-tree key-value stores, especially zone allocation, file placement and garbage collection for RocksDB on Zoned Namespace (ZNS) SSDs, and on caching Bloom filters for LSM-trees.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET in 2025. My undergraduate thesis, advised by Dr. Rifat Shahriyar, was on memory-efficient ML-based prefetch filtering.

Research Projects

Caching Bloom Filters for LSM-trees
Aug 2026 – Present
With Dr. Tarikul Islam Papon, Dr. Subhadeep Sarkar and Dr. Ju Hyoung Mun

  • Extending Modular Bloom Filters with new optimizations.
Extending Support for RocksDB on Zoned Namespace SSDs
Sept 2025 – Present
With Dr. Tarikul Islam Papon and Dr. Subhadeep Sarkar

Extending ZenFS with new algorithms and policies for:

  • New zone allocation: which zone to select for writes when a new zone is needed.
  • File placement: where to put a new SST file based on its properties.
  • Garbage collection: which files to move to make zone erasure possible.

We plan to publish a detailed experimental study to find the best configurations for a wide range of devices and workloads.

Memory-Efficient ML-based Prefetch Filtering (Undergraduate Thesis)
Feb 2024 – Mar 2025
Advisor: Dr. Rifat Shahriyar, BUET
  • Built a neural-network-based prefetch filter, with a training and testing pipeline in the ChampSim cache simulator, and compared it with the Perceptron-based filter PPF.
  • Cut model weights by 93% and total memory overhead by 47% while keeping performance close to the reference model.
  • Improved coverage over the baseline prefetcher without a significant loss of accuracy.
